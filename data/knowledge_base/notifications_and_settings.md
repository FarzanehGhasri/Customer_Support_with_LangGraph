# Notifications, language and appearance

**Keywords:** notification, email alerts, dark mode, theme, language, timezone

## Notifications

Configure per-channel notifications in **Settings -> Notifications**. Email
digests are sent at 08:00 in your account timezone; switch to instant delivery
or turn them off entirely from the same screen. Mobile push requires the system
permission to be granted to the app.

## Dark mode

**Settings -> Appearance -> Theme** offers Light, Dark and "Match system".

## Language and timezone

**Settings -> General** controls the interface language (14 languages available)
and the timezone used for timestamps and digests.
