# Two-factor authentication and account lockouts

**Keywords:** 2FA, two factor, authenticator, verification code, locked out, too many attempts

## Setting up 2FA

Go to **Settings -> Security -> Two-factor authentication -> Enable**, scan the
QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password), then
type the 6-digit code to confirm. Save the 10 recovery codes shown afterwards --
each one can be used once if you lose your device.

## Lost your authenticator device

Sign in with your email and password, choose **Use a recovery code**, and enter
one of the saved codes. If you have no recovery codes left, contact support from
the email address on the account; identity verification takes up to 24 hours.

## Account locked after too many attempts

Five failed sign-in attempts lock the account for **30 minutes**. The lock lifts
automatically; resetting your password also clears it immediately.
