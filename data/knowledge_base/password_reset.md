# Resetting your password

**Applies to:** web app, desktop app, mobile app
**Keywords:** password, reset password, forgot password, change password, cannot log in, login

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Open the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?** below the password field,
   or go directly to <https://app.example.com/reset-password>.
2. Enter the email address on your account and press **Send reset link**.
3. We email you a reset link that is valid for **60 minutes**. If it does not
   arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.
4. Click the link, type your new password twice, and press **Save**.
5. You are signed out of all devices and can sign in again with the new password.

## Change a password you still remember

Go to **Settings -> Security -> Change password**, enter the current password
followed by the new one, and press **Update**.

## Password requirements

* at least 10 characters
* at least one number and one letter
* must not repeat any of your last 3 passwords

## Reset link does not work

The link is single-use and expires after 60 minutes. Request a new one from the
same page. If you still cannot reset, your account may use single sign-on (SSO);
in that case the password is managed by your identity provider, not by us.
